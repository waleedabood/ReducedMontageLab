    How to use

    • Sweeper: the red line is shared by both montages. Click or drag in either to move it; hover to see a dashed guide at the same instant in the other.
    • Channels: hover a channel to light up its electrode pair on the head map.
    • Overview strip: click to seek; coloured marks are blinks (blue), eye opening/closure (amber) and spindles (green).
    • Keys: Space play/pause · ←/→ previous/next page · ,/. nudge the sweeper 0.2 s · ↑/↓ sensitivity.
    • Both montages share sensitivity and time base, so equal deflections are equal voltages. Negative is up.
